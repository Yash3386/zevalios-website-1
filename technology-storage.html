1. Current website technologies

The production website is designed without advertising pixels, behavioural tracking or analytics scripts. It stores a colour-mode preference locally in your browser when you use the light/dark toggle. This preference is used only to provide the interface setting you request.

2. Hosting and security

Netlify may use technical measures necessary to serve pages, protect forms, manage security and prevent abuse. Strictly necessary technologies may be used without consent where the applicable conditions are met.

3. No optional tracking at launch

No optional analytics, marketing or profiling technology is included in this codebase. Accordingly, the site does not display a consent banner in its current configuration.

4. Future changes

If optional analytics, embedded media, advertising or other non-essential technologies are introduced, the notice and consent mechanism must be updated before those technologies are activated.

5. Managing local storage

You can clear the saved colour preference through your browser settings. The site will then return to its default mode.
