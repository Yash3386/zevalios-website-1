Technology Storage Notice

Effective date: 28 July 2026

Legal informationPrivacy noticeTechnology storageTerms of useConsultancy disclaimer
The website contains no advertising pixels, behavioural tracking scripts or client-side analytics. It therefore does not display a consent banner in its launch configuration.

1. What this notice covers

This notice explains cookies, browser storage and comparable technologies used by the Zevalios website.

2. Colour-mode preference

When you select light or dark mode, the website stores the choice in your browser's local storage under the key zevalios-theme. This is used only to remember the interface preference you explicitly selected. It is not used for advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking.

3. Hosting and security data

Netlify serves the website and processes technical request information needed to deliver and secure it. Hosting infrastructure may use strictly necessary security mechanisms or server logs. Zevalios does not add non-essential tracking technologies through the site code.

4. Contact form

The contact form uses Netlify's form-processing infrastructure and anti-spam controls. The form does not depend on an advertising or analytics cookie.

5. Future changes

If analytics, embedded media, advertising or another non-essential technology is introduced, this notice and the consent mechanism will be reviewed before that technology is enabled.

6. Managing local storage

You may clear the colour preference through your browser's site-data settings. The site will then use the browser or device colour preference until you choose another mode.
