About

Commercially minded. Legally rigorous. Implementation-focused.

Zevalios exists to close the gap between what a business commits to, what operating teams deliver and what finance can invoice and verify.

Founder

Yashwantt Ramnarain

A Dublin-based corporate and commercial professional with more than seven years of experience across in-house and private-practice environments.

Yashwantt’s background includes corporate governance, commercial contracting, cross-border transactions, M&A, risk analysis and the practical translation of legal and commercial obligations into operating routines.

In-house experience exposed the recurring gap between a well-drafted commitment and a workflow that reliably assigns responsibility, captures evidence, triggers action and supports invoicing. Zevalios is built specifically around closing that gap.

  • Corporate and commercial experience
  • In-house operating perspective
  • Process, register and control design
  • English and French client capability

Professional boundaries

What Zevalios is—and is not.

Zevalios is a commercial operations optimisation consultancy. It applies contractual understanding, governance awareness and legal precision to business-process and control design.

Zevalios is not an Irish law firm, does not hold itself out as providing reserved legal services and does not replace the client’s external lawyers, accountants, technology specialists or regulated advisers.

Where an engagement identifies an issue requiring specialist legal, tax, cybersecurity, accounting or software-development advice, the client should obtain that advice from an appropriately qualified provider.
