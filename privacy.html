1. Scope

This notice explains how personal data is handled when you visit this website, contact Zevalios or submit an enquiry form. It is intended to provide the information required by the General Data Protection Regulation and applicable Irish data-protection law.

2. Data collected

Depending on your interaction, Zevalios may process your name, business email, company, role, optional telephone number, selected service areas, enquiry content and follow-up correspondence. The hosting provider may also process technical and security data such as Internet Protocol address, timestamp, browser information and anti-spam signals.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

Enquiry information is used to assess and respond to your request, arrange discussions, administer pre-contractual steps and protect the website from abuse. The principal lawful bases are taking steps at your request before entering a contract and Zevalios's legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, maintaining records and securing the website.

4. Recipients and service providers

Website hosting and form processing are provided through Netlify. Email and ordinary business-service providers may process information where necessary to respond to the enquiry or operate the business. Personal data is not sold.

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers should be supported by an adequacy decision, approved safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism. The final deployment configuration and provider terms should be reviewed before publication.

6. Retention

Unsuccessful or preliminary enquiries are normally retained for up to 24 months after the last substantive contact, unless a shorter or longer period is justified by the circumstances, legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of claims. Client records are governed by the applicable engagement terms and legal obligations.

7. Rights

Subject to applicable conditions and exemptions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

8. Required information

Required fields are identified in the form. Without them, Zevalios may be unable to assess or respond to the enquiry. Do not submit special-category, privileged or highly sensitive data through the initial form.

9. Automated decisions

The website does not use automated decision-making or profiling to determine whether an enquiry is accepted.

10. Updates

This notice may be updated when the business, website or processing practices change. Effective date: 29 July 2026.
