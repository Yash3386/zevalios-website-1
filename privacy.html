1. Scope

This notice explains how personal data is handled when you visit this website, contact Zevalios or submit the workflow discussion form. Zevalios applies the General Data Protection Regulation and applicable Irish data-protection law.

2. Personal data collected

Depending on how you interact with the website, the following data may be processed:

  • identity and business-contact data, such as your name, work email, company, role and optional telephone number;
  • the contents of your enquiry and any follow-up correspondence;
  • technical and security information generated when the site is served or a form is submitted, such as Internet Protocol address, timestamp, browser information and anti-spam signals processed by the hosting provider; and
  • your colour-mode preference, stored locally in your browser and not transmitted to Zevalios by the website code.

4. Recipients and processors

Personal data may be processed by service providers used to host the website, receive form submissions, provide email and maintain information-technology systems. The launch configuration uses Netlify for hosting and form processing. Service providers are required to process data under appropriate contractual and security arrangements.

5. International transfers

Some service providers or their subprocessors may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, Zevalios relies on an applicable transfer mechanism and supplementary safeguards where required, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention

Enquiry data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, assess a possible engagement, maintain proportionate business records and address legal or security issues. The relevant period depends on whether the enquiry becomes an engagement, whether the correspondence remains active and whether a legal retention requirement applies.

7. Your rights

Subject to the applicable conditions and exemptions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

To exercise a right, email yash@zevalios.com. Reasonable identity verification may be required.

8. Required and optional information

The form identifies which fields are required. Without the required information, Zevalios may be unable to assess or respond to the enquiry. The telephone field is optional. Please do not submit special-category, privileged or highly sensitive personal data through the initial form.

9. Automated decision-making

The website does not use automated decision-making or profiling to make decisions about enquiries.

10. Changes

This notice may be updated when the website, service providers or processing practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when a material update is made.

We identify where revenue, time and accountability are being lost, redesign the operating controls, implement the required tools and verify that the new system works.
