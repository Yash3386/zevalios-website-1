Service 02

Contract-to-Cash & Revenue Control

Strengthen the path from commercial commitment through delivery, evidence, invoicing, collection and renewal.

Discuss this service →

When clients use this service

The visible symptoms

  • Commercial commitments are not translated into delivery requirements.
  • Completed work is invoiced late, incompletely or only after intervention.
  • Invoice triggers and delivery evidence do not align.
  • Disputes, credit notes and collection delays repeat.
  • Management cannot see where earned value is blocked.

What Zevalios does

The intervention

  • Map the commitment-to-cash chain and identify leakage points.
  • Translate terms into owners, triggers, evidence and follow-up actions.
  • Redesign delivery-to-invoice handoffs and exception controls.
  • Implement obligation, evidence, billing and renewal trackers.
  • Test whether the controls improve visibility and execution.
