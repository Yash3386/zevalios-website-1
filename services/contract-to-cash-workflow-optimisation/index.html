Service

Contract-to-cash workflow optimisation

Many companies lose time and revenue between signature, onboarding, delivery, invoicing and collection. This service focuses on the process failures that delay cash conversion or allow obligations and billables to fall between teams.

What this service covers

  • Map the process from signed commitment to collection
  • Identify delayed billing triggers, missing evidence and unclear ownership
  • Redesign handoffs between sales, legal, operations and finance
  • Create controls that reduce invoice friction, leakage and follow-up burden

Typical outputs

Contract-to-cash workflow map

A practical view of the full process and its dependencies.

Billing-trigger controls

Clear triggers, evidence and ownership for timely invoicing.

Exception and escalation logic

A defined route for resolving delays and incomplete information.

Implementation plan

Actions, priorities and responsibilities for putting the redesigned process in place.

When it is useful

This service is most relevant where execution depends on coordination between functions, where there are repeat delays or rework, or where management wants stronger revenue control without overcomplicating operations.
