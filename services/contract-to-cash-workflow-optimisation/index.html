Contract-to-cash workflow optimisation

Many companies lose time and revenue between signature, onboarding, delivery, invoicing and collection. This service focuses on the specific process failures that delay cash conversion or allow obligations and billables to fall between teams.

What this service covers

  • Identify where signed commitments are not being translated into operational action
  • Reduce onboarding delays, delivery slippage and invoicing friction
  • Strengthen billing discipline, handoff clarity and evidence capture
  • Improve visibility on stuck items, revenue leakage and follow-up ownership
