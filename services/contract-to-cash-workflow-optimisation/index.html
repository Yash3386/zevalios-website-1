Contract-to-cash workflow optimisation

Many companies lose time and revenue between signature, onboarding, delivery, invoicing and collection. This service focuses on the specific process failures that delay cash conversion or allow obligations and billables to fall between teams.

What this service covers

  • Map the journey from signed commitment to cash collection.
  • Identify missed handoffs, delayed billing triggers and obligations that disappear between teams.
  • Redesign controls around onboarding, delivery confirmation, invoicing and follow-up.
  • Reduce leakage, chasing and unnecessary management involvement.

Typical outputs

Workflow map

A practical view of the steps, dependencies and failure points.

Billing control logic

Defined triggers, checkpoints and ownership rules for invoicing.

Exception handling

A route for dealing with delays, gaps and special cases.

Operational recommendations

A practical improvement plan grounded in how the company actually works.

When it is useful

This service is most relevant where signed work takes too long to turn into cash, where billables are inconsistently captured, or where finance and operations spend too much time chasing information.

Discuss this service

We identify where revenue, time and accountability are being lost, redesign the operating controls, implement the required tools and verify that the new system works.
