Services

Operating controls that connect commercial commitment to cash.

Zevalios combines diagnosis, process design, implementation and verification. The service is deliberately narrower and more accountable than a generic transformation programme.

01

Commercial Workflow Diagnostic

Map one high-impact process, identify leakage points, determine root causes and define the control architecture required to correct them.

  • Stakeholder interviews and process walkthrough
  • Sample contract, order, delivery and invoice review
  • Failure-point and accountability analysis
  • Prioritised implementation roadmap

02

Operating Model & Control Design

Convert findings into a future-state process with explicit owners, decision rights, evidence requirements and management visibility.

  • Future-state workflow
  • Responsibility and decision-rights matrix
  • Approval and escalation rules
  • Control matrix and indicators

03

Workflow Implementation

Implement the operating system through the client’s existing tools or an appropriate third-party platform where justified.

  • Templates, registers and checklists
  • CRM and workflow configuration where appropriate
  • Dashboards and exception reporting
  • Standard operating procedures and training

04

Verification & Stabilisation

Test whether the redesigned controls operate in practice and correct weaknesses before final handover.

  • User and control testing
  • Data-quality and adoption review
  • Indicator and exception analysis
  • Verification report and next-step roadmap

Common process chains

One critical workflow first.

Scope is selected by economic importance, operational pain and the availability of evidence—not by the desire to redesign everything at once.

Quotation to cash

Lead ownership, proposal follow-up, contract activation, delivery evidence, invoicing and collections handoff.

Contract to delivery

Obligation extraction, onboarding, responsibilities, milestones, approvals, variation control and completion evidence.

Delivery to invoice

Completion triggers, evidence requirements, billing readiness, exception resolution, underbilling and late invoicing.

Start with the workflow that has the clearest commercial consequence.
