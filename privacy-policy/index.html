Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Zevalios may collect, use and protect personal data through this website.

1. Data we may collect

  • Contact details you submit through the website, such as name, email address, phone number and company details.
  • Message content or business information you choose to provide.
  • Basic technical and usage data, such as browser type, device information, pages viewed and approximate traffic data.

2. How we use personal data

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • To assess whether our services may be relevant to your needs.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the website.
  • To protect the website and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Legal basis

Depending on the context, processing may be based on legitimate interests, steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required.

4. Sharing of data

Zevalios does not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with service providers involved in website hosting, analytics, email or operational support, subject to appropriate safeguards and only as reasonably necessary.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law, regulation or legitimate business needs.

6. International transfers

If personal data is transferred outside your jurisdiction, Zevalios will seek to ensure that appropriate protections are in place where legally required.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, and in some cases receive a copy of your data.

8. Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality and basic performance measurement. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to help protect personal data, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact info@zevalios.com.
