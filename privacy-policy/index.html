Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. Who controls your personal data

For this website, the data controller is Yashwantt Ramnarain trading as Zevalios, Dublin, Ireland. Contact: yash@zevalios.com.

2. Information collected

Zevalios may collect the information you submit through the enquiry form or by email, including your name, business email, company, role, telephone number, selected service areas and the information included in your message. The hosting provider may also process technical information required to deliver and secure the website, such as Internet Protocol address, device and browser information, timestamps and security logs.

3. Why the information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and determine whether Zevalios may be a suitable consultancy provider.
  • To communicate about a requested discussion or proposed engagement.
  • To maintain website security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, accounting or dispute-resolution obligations.

4. Lawful bases

Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and operating a secure consultancy website, consent where specifically requested, or compliance with a legal obligation.

5. Service providers and international transfers

The site is hosted through Netlify. Email and other business systems may process information on Zevalios’s behalf. Providers may process data outside Ireland or the European Economic Area. Where required, Zevalios will rely on an applicable transfer mechanism and contractual safeguards.

6. Retention

Unsuccessful or preliminary enquiries are generally retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain an appropriate record and address follow-up, normally no longer than 24 months unless a longer period is justified. Engagement records may be retained for the duration of the relationship and afterwards for applicable legal, accounting, insurance and limitation periods.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

8. Security and confidentiality

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. No internet transmission is completely secure. Do not submit confidential, privileged, special-category or highly sensitive information through the initial enquiry form.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, services, providers or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
