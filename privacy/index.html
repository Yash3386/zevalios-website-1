Zevalios is the controller of personal data submitted through this website. Contact: info@zevalios.com.

The contact form may collect your name, work email address, company, telephone number and the high-level description you choose to provide.

3. Purpose and legal basis

The data is used to assess and respond to enquiries, take steps at your request before a possible engagement, and manage legitimate business communications.

Netlify processes form and hosting data on behalf of Zevalios. The selected business-email provider processes correspondence. Their role and international-transfer arrangements must be confirmed before final publication.

5. Retention

Enquiry data should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage follow-up and comply with legal obligations. The final retention period must be documented before launch.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability and may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

7. Sensitive information

Do not submit confidential, legally privileged, security-sensitive or special-category personal data through the website form.

8. Cookies and analytics

The launch version does not deploy advertising trackers, behavioural analytics or non-essential cookies.
