Website privacy notice

How Zevalios handles information submitted through this website or sent directly by email.

Internal launch requirement: Review this notice against the final email provider, Netlify configuration, retention practice and legal entity details before public launch.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

1. Who controls your information

Zevalios, Dublin, Ireland, is responsible for the personal data described in this notice. Privacy enquiries may be sent to privacy@zevalios.com.

2. Information collected

The website may collect the information you voluntarily submit through the contact form or by email, including your name, work contact details, company, role and the business issue you describe. Basic technical logs may also be processed by the hosting provider for security, reliability and service operation.

3. Why information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and assess whether Zevalios can assist.
  • To arrange and prepare for calls or proposed engagements.
  • To maintain appropriate business, security and compliance records.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

The principal legal bases are steps taken at your request before a possible contract, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the business, compliance with legal obligations and, where applicable, consent.

4. Do not submit sensitive information

Do not submit confidential contracts, legally privileged material, credentials, security-sensitive information, health information or other special-category personal data through the website form. Appropriate transfer and confidentiality arrangements should be agreed before such material is shared.

5. Service providers

The website is intended to be hosted by Netlify, and business email is intended to be provided through Google Workspace. These providers may process limited information to deliver hosting, form and email services under their own contractual and security arrangements. The final provider configuration must be confirmed before launch.

6. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Where required, the provider and Zevalios rely on recognised transfer mechanisms and safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention

Enquiry information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the potential business relationship, meet legal or compliance requirements and resolve disputes. Unsuccessful or inactive enquiries should be periodically reviewed and deleted where no longer required.

8. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, obtain data portability and lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission or another competent supervisory authority.

9. Cookies and analytics

The initial website is designed without advertising trackers, behavioural profiling or non-essential analytics. Essential hosting and security technologies may still be used where necessary to operate and protect the site.

10. Changes

This notice may be updated when the website, providers or processing activities change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.
