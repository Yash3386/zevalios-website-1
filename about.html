About Zevalios

Commercially minded. Legally rigorous. Built for implementation.

Zevalios exists to bring clarity and control to the commercial work a business depends on.

Operating premise

Most recurring business failures are not caused by a lack of effort.

They are caused by unclear ownership, weak evidence, undocumented decisions, fragmented handoffs and controls that depend on memory or repeated senior intervention.

What Zevalios changes

Make the work executable, visible and verifiable.

Each engagement converts complexity into workflows, responsibilities, controls, documents and operating tools that people can actually use.

Founder

Yashwantt Ramnarain

A corporate and commercial legal background combined with in-house operational judgement.

Yashwantt has more than seven years of experience across corporate, Mergers and Acquisitions, commercial contracts, governance, risk and cross-border transactions. His work has consistently required him to translate complex obligations and commercial objectives into processes, controls, decisions and follow-up structures.

At Promethera Biosciences and Cellaïon, he worked directly with senior leadership, including Chief Executive, Finance and Operations functions. He held team-lead responsibility across legal and commercial workstreams and contributed to crisis-management execution, where clarity, prioritisation and reliable follow-through were essential.

That combination—legal precision, commercial judgement and systems thinking—is the core of Zevalios.

Credibility through execution

Comfortable where legal, commercial, financial and operational decisions meet.

Zevalios is designed for problems that do not sit neatly inside one function and cannot be solved by producing another isolated document.

Sturdiness

Controls should not work only when the founder, manager or expert is watching.

They should survive growth, absence, turnover, increased volume, cross-border complexity and ordinary operational pressure.
