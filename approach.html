Control standard

Every material finding must be traceable.

A finding without a source, owner, impact and verification test is not a usable control finding. The design standard keeps the intervention tied to evidence rather than opinion.

SOURCE

What proves the problem exists?

Contract, document, system record, transaction sample, interview evidence or observed process behaviour.

OWNER

Who is accountable for the action?

One clearly identified role, with decision rights and a defined route when the owner is unavailable.

IMPACT

What commercial consequence follows?

Delay, lost revenue, weak evidence, dispute exposure, management dependency, rework or poor visibility.

TEST

How will the control be verified?

A repeatable operating test that shows whether the redesigned control works in real conditions.

Fit

The model works only where operating change is genuinely wanted.

Relevant where

  • Revenue depends on recurring, milestone or project-based invoicing.
  • Commitments cross several teams, systems, entities or jurisdictions.
  • Invoicing depends on evidence from delivery.
  • Follow-up relies on manual chasing.
  • Ownership is unclear at approvals, exceptions or handoffs.
  • Management suspects leakage but cannot see where it occurs.

Not a fit where

  • The buyer wants only a motivational workshop.
  • There is no internal owner able to implement change.
  • The company wants a report but no operating change.
  • The issue is purely jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

We identify where revenue, time and accountability are being lost, redesign the operating controls, implement the required tools and verify that the new system works.
