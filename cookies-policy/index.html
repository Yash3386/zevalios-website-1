Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Zevalios uses cookies and similar browser-storage technologies on this website.

Last updated: 28 July 2026.

1. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can remember choices without necessarily creating a traditional cookie.

2. Technologies currently used

This website currently uses essential browser storage to remember your day or night display preference and your cookie-consent choice. These functions are required to provide the preferences you select.

3. Optional cookies

Optional analytics, advertising or profiling cookies are not activated by this website at present. The consent panel allows you to accept or reject optional cookies so that your preference is recorded if optional tools are introduced later.

4. Your choice

You may accept or reject optional cookies through the consent panel. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent you from using the website's core functions.

5. Changing or clearing your preference

You can clear this website's stored data through your browser settings. The consent panel will then be shown again on your next visit. Browser controls can also block or delete cookies and local storage.

6. Third-party services

If Zevalios later introduces analytics, embedded media or other third-party services that use optional cookies, this policy and the consent mechanism should be updated before those services are activated.

7. Contact

For questions about cookies or privacy, contact info@zevalios.com.

We identify where revenue, time and accountability are being lost, redesign the operating controls, implement the required tools and verify that the new system works.
