Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. Current use of cookies and browser storage

This website currently does not deploy analytics or advertising cookies. It uses limited browser storage to remember your chosen day/night theme and your cookie-preference response. Those functions are treated as necessary to provide the choices you request and to avoid repeatedly displaying the same notice.

3. Optional technologies

No optional analytics, advertising or behavioural-profiling technologies are active at the date shown above. They will not be introduced without updating this policy and, where required, obtaining consent before activation.

4. Google Fonts

The website loads typography from Google Fonts. This may cause your browser to request font files from Google and disclose technical connection information such as your Internet Protocol address. Google Fonts are not used by Zevalios for advertising or analytics on this site.

5. Managing preferences

You can reopen the preference panel through the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. You can also clear local storage through your browser settings. Blocking necessary storage may prevent the website from remembering your selected theme or preference.
