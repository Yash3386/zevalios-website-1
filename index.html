01 — 05 The hidden commercial leak

Small failures.
Material loss.

Revenue is often lost after the sale—between contract signature, service delivery and invoicing. The problem is rarely one dramatic failure. It is repeated friction across ownership, evidence, handovers, approvals and billing.

01

Time leakage

Rework, chasing, manual updates, duplicate entry and slow handovers.

02

Accountability leakage

No single owner, incomplete evidence and escalation after the deadline.

03

Revenue leakage

Missed billables, delayed invoicing, weak variation and renewal discipline.

“The business may have capable people and good software. The loss sits in the operating chain between them.”
