Where value is lost

The most expensive problems often look ordinary.

A missed handoff, an unowned approval, a weak evidence trail, an inconsistent template or a cross-border workstream without clear coordination can quietly multiply delay, rework and commercial risk.

Commitments remain buriedContracts, emails and decisions do not become visible actions, owners and deadlines.
Handoffs lose accountabilityWork moves between sales, delivery, finance, legal and management without clear triggers or escalation.
Manual follow-up becomes the control systemCritical tasks depend on memory, repeated chasing and individual workarounds.
Documents do not drive executionTemplates, registers and evidence are inconsistent, incomplete or disconnected from the real workflow.
